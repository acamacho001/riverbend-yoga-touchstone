Our Instructors

Christen Olsten

Christen Olsten is a Riverbend Yoga Studio instructor who focuses on beginner-friendly movement, gentle yoga, and restorative practices. Olsten encourages students to work at their own pace and offers modifications to support different mobility and experience levels.

Accessibility Accommodations

Riverbend Yoga Studio welcomes students with different mobility and experience levels. Instructors can provide modified poses and slower class options when appropriate.

Students are encouraged to contact the studio before attending if they have accessibility needs or questions about a class.

Studio Policies

Students should arrive a few minutes before class, respect the comfort and space of other participants, and inform the instructor of any limitations that may affect their practice.

Riverbend encourages a respectful and welcoming environment where students can practice at their own pace.
