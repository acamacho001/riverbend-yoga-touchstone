Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

Riverbend Yoga Studio provides a calm, inclusive, and supportive environment for people who want to improve movement, flexibility, strength, and relaxation.

No previous yoga experience or advanced flexibility is required. Our classes are designed to support beginners, busy professionals, older adults, and experienced students.

Featured Class

Gentle Yoga

Gentle Yoga uses slower movements, comfortable poses, and breathing exercises to help students improve mobility and reduce stress. It is especially suitable for beginners and students who prefer a lower-intensity practice.

Schedule Overview

Riverbend offers morning, afternoon, and evening classes throughout the week. Classes include Gentle Yoga, Vinyasa, Restorative Yoga, private sessions, and monthly workshops.
