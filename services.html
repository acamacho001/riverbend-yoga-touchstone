Yoga Classes

Gentle Yoga

A slower-paced class focused on mobility, breathing, and comfortable movement. This class is especially suitable for beginners and older adults.

Vinyasa Yoga

A more active class that connects movement and breathing through a flowing sequence of yoga poses.

Restorative Yoga

A calming practice using supported poses and longer periods of rest to encourage relaxation and stress reduction.

Private Sessions

Private sessions provide personalized instruction based on individual goals, experience level, mobility needs, and comfort.

Pricing Overview

Riverbend Yoga Studio offers single-class rates, multi-class packages, and private-session options. Contact the studio for current pricing and availability.
